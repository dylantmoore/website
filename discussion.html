Predistribution & Equality of Opportunity

  • Earnings: $$z=A\,\omega(1-\tau(z))^{\varepsilon}$$
  • Skill depends on background: $$\omega=\Omega(\omega_0,E(\omega_0))$$
  • Redistribution rule: $$\frac{\tau(z)}{1-\tau(z)}=\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\frac{1-H(z)}{z h(z)}(1-G(z,A^*))$$
  • Education FOC: $$[E(\partial\tau/\partial z \cdot \partial z/\partial \omega)+g(\omega_0)MWP]\,\Omega'_E=p$$
  • When to favor predistribution: large \(\varepsilon\), elastic \(\Omega'_E\), low crowd-out

Optimal Public Goods Spending

  • Public good \(G\) financed by nonlinear taxes
  • Optimality equates social MWTP to marginal cost:
  • $$\sum_i g_i\,\text{MWTP}_i(G)=p_G$$
  • Each agent’s MWTP derived from indirect utility and tax incidence
  • Normative weight \(g_i\) adjusts with \(\lambda\): Lindahl shares vs. utilitarian weights
  • Interpretation: unifies benefit-based justification with welfare-maximizing benchmark
